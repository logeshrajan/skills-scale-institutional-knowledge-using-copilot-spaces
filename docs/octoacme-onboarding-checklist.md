# OctoAcme — Onboarding Checklist

## Purpose
Provide a structured, comprehensive checklist for onboarding new team members to ensure consistent knowledge transfer and rapid productivity.

## For: Onboarding Coordinators and New Team Members

## Pre-Arrival (Onboarding Coordinator)
- [ ] Create accounts for email, chat, and collaboration tools
- [ ] Set up access to code repositories and project management systems
- [ ] Prepare workstation or remote setup instructions
- [ ] Assign onboarding buddy/mentor
- [ ] Schedule orientation meetings and training sessions
- [ ] Send welcome email with first-day logistics

## Day 1: Orientation
- [ ] Complete IT setup and verify access to all systems
- [ ] Review company values, mission, and organizational structure
- [ ] Meet with manager to discuss role expectations and initial goals
- [ ] Meet onboarding buddy/mentor
- [ ] Tour of office (if applicable) or virtual team introductions
- [ ] Review security policies and sign required documents

## Week 1: Process and Documentation
- [ ] Read central project management documentation (docs/README.md)
- [ ] Review OctoAcme Project Management Overview
- [ ] Study role-specific documentation from octoacme-roles-and-personas.md
- [ ] Explore Copilot Spaces and locate key knowledge resources
- [ ] Attend team standup and observe team communication patterns
- [ ] Shadow team members in your role for 2-3 sessions
- [ ] Complete assigned tutorials or training modules

## Week 2: Engagement and Contribution
- [ ] Participate in sprint planning or project kickoff meeting
- [ ] Take on first starter task or "good first issue"
- [ ] Make first code or documentation contribution
- [ ] Attend retrospective or team feedback session
- [ ] Review project boards and understand current priorities
- [ ] Schedule 1:1 meetings with key cross-functional partners
- [ ] Begin participating actively in code reviews or document reviews

## Month 1: Integration
- [ ] Deliver first significant contribution or feature
- [ ] Present work or learnings in team meeting
- [ ] Provide feedback on onboarding process to Onboarding Coordinator
- [ ] Complete any required certifications or compliance training
- [ ] Establish regular communication cadence with manager and team
- [ ] Understand escalation paths and risk communication procedures

## 30-Day Feedback Checkpoint
- [ ] Schedule 30-day check-in with manager
- [ ] Complete onboarding feedback survey
- [ ] Identify areas for additional support or training
- [ ] Set goals for the next 60-90 days

## Resources for New Team Members
- **Project Management Docs:** `/docs/` directory
- **Role Definitions:** `docs/octoacme-roles-and-personas.md`
- **Process Overview:** `docs/octoacme-project-management-overview.md`
- **Communication Guidelines:** `docs/octoacme-risks-and-communication.md`
- **Copilot Spaces:** Access institutional knowledge and process context
- **Onboarding Buddy:** Your assigned mentor for questions and guidance

## For Onboarding Coordinators: Continuous Improvement
- Collect feedback from each new hire at 30, 60, and 90 days
- Update this checklist based on common gaps or confusion
- Share onboarding metrics with leadership quarterly
- Coordinate with Knowledge Curators to keep documentation current
- Recognize and celebrate successful onboarding milestones
